## English

# Privacy Policy — Former

**Last updated:** November 19, 2026.

Former is a browser extension that blocks access to informer.rs and selected associated social media accounts.

## What information does Former process?

Former temporarily checks the URLs of opened pages and the destinations of certain links on YouTube, entirely within the user's browser. This check is performed only to determine whether a URL should be redirected to the “ACCESS DENIED” page.

## Data collection and sharing

Former does not send these URLs to the extension's developer or to any server operated by the developer. It does not store browsing history or use analytics, user tracking, or advertising systems. The extension does not request access to passwords or cookies. Blocked-URL checks are performed locally.

## Permissions

Former uses the `declarativeNetRequest` permission for local redirect rules and `webNavigation` to detect certain URL changes within social media platforms. Access to the listed domains is used solely to identify and block selected accounts.

The `blocked.html` page is declared as a web-accessible resource so that redirects from other websites work correctly. This does not allow the extension to read all websites, but it may allow other websites to check whether that resource exists.

## Changes to this policy

If the extension's functionality or data-handling practices change, we will update this document and, where necessary, the notices in extension stores.

## Srpski

# Politika privatnosti — Former

**Poslednje ažuriranje:** 19. novembar 2026.

Former je ekstenzija za pregledač koja blokira pristup sajtu informer.rs i odabranim nalozima povezanim sa njim na društvenim mrežama.

## Koje podatke Former obrađuje?

Former privremeno, isključivo unutar korisnikovog pregledača, proverava adrese (URL-ove) otvorenih stranica i odredišta određenih linkova na YouTube-u. Ta provera služi samo da utvrdi da li adresu treba preusmeriti na stranicu „PRISTUP ODBIJEN“.

## Prikupljanje i deljenje podataka

Former ne šalje te adrese autoru ekstenzije niti bilo kom njegovom serveru, ne čuva istoriju pregledanja i ne koristi analitiku, praćenje korisnika ili reklamne sisteme. Ekstenzija ne traži pristup lozinkama ili kolačićima. Provera blokiranih adresa izvršava se lokalno.

## Dozvole

Former koristi dozvolu `declarativeNetRequest` za lokalna pravila preusmeravanja i `webNavigation` za prepoznavanje određenih promena adrese unutar društvenih mreža. Pristup navedenim domenima služi isključivo da prepozna i blokira izabrane naloge.

Stranica `blocked.html` je označena kao web-dostupan resurs kako bi preusmeravanje radilo iz drugih sajtova. To ne omogućava ekstenziji da čita sve sajtove, ali drugim sajtovima može omogućiti da provere postojanje tog resursa.
